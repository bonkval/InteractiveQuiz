Cookie Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Rev does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies. It uses browser storage for the reviewer, study progress, display preference, and account session features you choose to use.

Device storage

Reviewers, exhibits, answers, flags, history, prompts, and preferences are saved in local storage on this browser. Supabase may also store an authentication session in browser storage when you sign in. This storage is used to provide the features you request.

Choices

You can study without signing in. You can remove Rev’s local study data in Data & deletion or through your browser settings. Signing out removes your current account session. Since Rev does not currently use optional tracking, it does not show a cookie consent banner.

Changes

If optional analytics or advertising storage is added later, Rev will update this page and ask for the consent required before activating it.
