Using Rev

Rev is a study tool for importing and reviewing questions. You can use its local study features without creating an account. If you choose to create an account, provide an email address you control and keep your sign-in details private.

Your materials

You are responsible for the questions and images you import and for having permission to use them. Rev does not review or endorse imported material. Parsing, answer keys, and scores can be inaccurate, so check important material against your source.

Availability and backups

Local browser data can be lost if storage is cleared or the device fails. Use Rev’s Backup feature to keep a copy of your reviewers. Online account and hosting features may be unavailable during outages or maintenance.

Accounts and deletion

Accounts currently separate local libraries on the same browser; they do not transfer reviewers between devices. The Data & deletion page provides local deletion and, on a configured hosted deployment, account deletion.
