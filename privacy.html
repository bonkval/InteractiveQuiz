Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Rev lets you review without an account. Your questions, exhibits, study progress, flags, and prompts are stored in this browser. Signing in currently does not sync reviewers between devices.

What Rev stores

Rev stores imported reviewers, attached exhibits, answers, progress, flags, study history, theme preferences, and edited prompts on your device. You can export a backup and delete the local data in Data & deletion.

Accounts

If you create an account, Supabase processes your email address, password, sign-in session, and the date and version of your terms acceptance to provide authentication. Rev does not store your password in its reviewer data. Account-specific reviewer libraries remain on the current device until cloud reviewer storage is introduced. You can delete your account from Data & deletion on a configured hosted deployment.

Hosting and other services

When you use a hosted version, Vercel delivers the site and may process request information needed to operate it. Supabase processes account data when authentication is enabled. Rev does not currently use advertising, analytics, or marketing trackers. PDF reading code and fonts are served with the app. If you import an exhibit by an external image URL, displaying it may contact that image host; attach a local image file to avoid that request.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains in your browser until you remove it, clear browser storage, or delete the browser profile. Backup files you export remain wherever you save them. An account remains with Supabase until it is deleted. The in-app deletion controls explain which data each action removes.

Changes

This page will be updated before Rev adds cloud reviewer syncing or optional tracking. Review the effective date for changes.
